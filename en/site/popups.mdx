A **popup (نافذة منبثقة)** is a small card that opens on top of a page of your store, at the moment you choose. The visitor sees it without looking for it, and closes it with one tap when done.

You'll find popups in your **site dashboard**, under **Store design (تصميم المتجر) → Popups (النوافذ المنبثقة)**.

<Info>
  Popups live in your **site dashboard** — the place you land when you sign in. Your Glowply billing is separate; see [Your account & billing](/en/account/overview). New here? See [Site dashboard overview](/en/site/dashboard-overview).
</Info>

<Tip>
  The Popups page itself opens with a short illustrated guide, **What are popups? (إيه هي النوافذ المنبثقة؟)**. Choose **Hide the guide (اخفي الشرح)** once you've read it, and **Show the guide (اعرض الشرح)** to bring it back. Your choice is remembered.
</Tip>

## What popups are good for

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="An offer or a discount code" icon="tag">
    Show a discount on the first order and nudge the visitor to buy.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collect numbers and e-mails" icon="envelope">
    Ask the visitor for a phone number or e-mail so you can send offers later.
  </Card>
  <Card title="An important notice" icon="bullhorn">

## Make a popup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a popup">
    On the Popups page, press **Add Popup (ضيف نافذة منبثقة)** and give it a name you will recognise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it shows">
    Under **Location (الموقع)**, use **Apply popup to... (طبّق النافذة المنبثقة على...)**: every page of the store, or only some, like the home page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it shows">
    Under **Popup Opens When (النافذة المنبثقة بتفتح لما)**, press **Add Trigger (ضيف مُفعّل)** and pick the moment from the [list below](#when-a-popup-can-appear).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design it and save">
    Press **Edit in Glowply (عدّل في Glowply)**. The popup opens in the **Builder**, where you write the words and add the picture and the button, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When a popup can appear

You choose the moment with a **trigger**. These are the ones you can pick:

| The moment | Its name in the list |
|---|---|
| As soon as the page opens | **Page Load (تحميل الصفحة)** |
| After the visitor scrolls down | **Page Scroll (تمرير الصفحة)** |
| When they tap a button or a picture | **Click (نقر)** |
| When they are about to leave | **Mouse Moves to Exit (تحريك المؤشر نحو الخروج)** |
| When they stop for a while | **User Inactivity (خمول المستخدم)** |

<Note>
  **Mouse Moves to Exit** only works on a computer: a phone has no mouse pointer. If most of your visitors are on phones, choose another moment.
</Note>

## Keep it from annoying your visitors

- **One popup is enough.** More than one in a visit makes people leave.
- **Limit how often it shows.** Turn on **Limit how often this popup shows (حدّد معدّل ظهور النافذة المنبثقة دي)** so the same visitor doesn't get it on every page.
- **Few words and one clear button.** A visitor decides in two seconds.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a popup at all?">
    No. Your store works without one. Add a popup when you have something specific to say — an offer, a sign-up, an announcement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I show a popup on one page only?">
    Yes. In **Location (الموقع)**, apply the popup to that page instead of the whole store.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I stop a popup?">
    Open it from the Popups page and change where it applies, or delete it. It stops appearing on your store.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do the e-mails and numbers go?">
    A form inside a popup works like any form on your site: what visitors send arrives in **Customers (العملاء) → Customer messages (رسائل العملاء)**. See [Form submissions](/en/store/form-submissions).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
