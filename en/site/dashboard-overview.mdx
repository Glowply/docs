Your **site dashboard** is the control center for your website and online store. It's where you design pages, add products, take orders, manage billing, and grow your business — all in one place, available in **Arabic** and **English** (switch any time with the language toggle at the top).

<Info>
  **One place for everything.** Your store, your pages, your marketing, and your **subscription and billing** all live in your site dashboard. See [Your account & billing](/en/account/overview).
</Info>

## Open your site dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in at glowply.com">
    Go to glowply.com and sign in with your email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You land in your dashboard">
    Glowply takes you straight to your site dashboard — there's no second login. (Old `/my-account/` links forward here too.)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  At the top of the dashboard you'll find the green **View your store (شوف متجرك)** button to open your live site, the **عربي / EN** language toggle, and a light/dark switch. Bookmark the dashboard to come straight back next time.
</Tip>

## The dashboard home

Your dashboard home welcomes you and gives you a quick read on how your store is doing.

### Get your store ready for its first order

New stores show a **setup checklist** — **seven short steps** that take your store from its design to its first order. Work through it from top to bottom:

1. **Your design** — pick a ready-made design and make it yours (images, text, colours, and which sections show).
2. **Your logo** — upload your store's logo.
3. **Categories** — set up the categories you'll group your products into.
4. **Products** — add your first products. *An image, a name and a price — the rest can wait.*
5. **Delivery** — set where you deliver and what it costs.
6. **Test order** — place a practice order to see the buying flow end to end.
7. **Share link** — share your store's link and start selling.

Each step shows roughly how long it takes, and a counter at the top (for example **3 of 7 done**) tracks your progress. Tap a step to open where you complete it, then come back to the checklist.

<Note>
  The checklist is a guide, not a lock. You can explore the dashboard freely and come back to any step later.
</Note>

### Your store at a glance

Below the checklist, the home shows live numbers so you can see how your store is performing without opening any reports:

- **Store stats** for a date range (**Last 7 days** by default): **Total Revenue**, **Total Orders**, **Total Items Sold**, and **Avg. Order Value**, each with its change over the period.
- **New Orders** — your most recent orders, with **View All** to open the full list.
- **Top Selling Products** — what customers are buying most, with **View Reports** for the detail.

You'll also find quick buttons to **Add Product**, open **Orders** or **Products**, and create a **New Coupon**.

<Tip>
  Want a custom site built for you? The **"Send us photos and details of your business"** card offers a website designed just for you **in under 24 hours** — send your details and the team takes it from there.
</Tip>

## Ask Glowply — your in-dashboard assistant

Your dashboard has a built-in assistant, **Ask Glowply (اسأل glowply)**. Open it from the chat bubble in the corner, or from **Ask Glowply** in the menu, and ask any question about your store — "how do I add a product?", "where do I set delivery?" — and it answers from the Glowply guide.

<Note>
  Ask Glowply answers from Glowply's help guide and can occasionally be wrong. For anything it can't settle, reach a human on WhatsApp or by email — see [Contact support](/en/contact-support).
</Note>

## The left-side menu

A menu down the side of the dashboard groups everything into sections. Here's what each one is for:

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home page (الصفحة الرئيسية)** | The home screen — your setup checklist, store stats, and recent orders. |
| **Orders (الطلبات)** | Every order that comes in, and working it through to delivery. |
| **Store (المتجر)** | Your store essentials: **Payments (الدفع)** and **Shipping & delivery (الشحن)**. |
| **Products (المنتجات)** | Your catalogue: products, **Categories (الأقسام)**, **Product options (خيارات المنتجات)**, **Reviews (التقييمات)**, and **Discounts (الخصومات)**. |
| **Customers (العملاء)** | Your customers, and the **messages (رسائل العملاء)** they send through your site's forms. |
| **Analytics (التحليلات)** | Reports on revenue, orders, products, categories, coupons, and more. |
| **Branding & contact buttons (الهوية وأزرار التواصل)** | Your logo and store branding, and the contact buttons (WhatsApp and the like) shown across your site. |
| **Store design (تصميم المتجر)** | Build and arrange your site: **Pages (الصفحات)**, **Ready-made templates (القوالب الجاهزة)**, and **Popups (النوافذ المنبثقة)**. |
| **Content (المحتوى)** | Your **Blog (المدونة)** and your **images & files (الصور والملفات)**. |
| **Marketing (التسويق)** | Grow your store: **SEO (الظهور في جوجل)**, **Live Events (اللي بيحصل دلوقتي)**, **Journeys (رحلة العميل)**, **Website Analytics (زوار الموقع)**, and **Connect your ads (ربط الإعلانات)**. |
| **Settings (الإعدادات)** | **General settings (الإعدادات العامة)**, **Translate your site (ترجمة موقعك)**, and **Subscription & Billing (الاشتراك والفواتير)**. |

<Note>
  Your pages are designed in the **Builder (البلدر)** — Glowply's drag-and-drop visual editor. You reach it by editing a page under **Store design → Pages**. You don't need to write any code.
</Note>
