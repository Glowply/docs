Your **site dashboard** is the control center for your website and online store. It's your own site's admin area, in Arabic, where you design pages, add products, take orders, and grow your business. Your Glowply billing lives here too, under **Settings → Subscription & Billing** — see [Your account & billing](/en/account/overview).

<Info>
  **One place:** your **site dashboard (لوحة تحكم موقعك)** is where you build your site, run your store, and manage your billing under **Settings → Subscription & Billing**. See [Your account & billing](/en/account/overview).
</Info>

## Open your site dashboard

Your site dashboard opens automatically when you sign in — there's no second login.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in at glowply.com">
    Sign in at glowply.com with your email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Glowply dashboard">
    On your site card, choose **Glowply dashboard**. Glowply signs you in securely and opens your dashboard in a new browser tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start working on your site">
    You land on the dashboard home, with everything you need to set up and manage your site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Bookmark your dashboard so you can come straight back to it next time.
</Tip>

## The dashboard home

The first screen you see is your dashboard home. It welcomes you and gives you a quick read on how your site and store are doing.

### Quick tour to set up your store

Until your store is ready, the top of your Home page shows **Get your store ready for its first order (جهّز متجرك لأول طلب)**: seven short steps, a count such as "3 of 7 done", and your next step as a card with its button and about how long it takes. Each step opens a page that's already in your dashboard:

1. **Your design (التصميم)** — **Browse designs** opens the [Template library](/en/site/templates-library). Keep the design you have or switch to a ready-made one; the step counts once you've opened the library.
2. **Your logo (اللوجو)** — upload your logo. No logo yet? Use your store's name in its place.
3. **Categories (الأقسام)** — add at least one category, so customers find what they want fast. See [Categories & tags](/en/store/categories-tags).
4. **Products (المنتجات)** — add a product. An image, a name and a price are enough to start; the step counts once one of your own products is published. See [Products](/en/store/products).
5. **Delivery (التوصيل)** — set what delivery costs and where you deliver. See [Shipping](/en/store/shipping).
6. **Test order (أوردر تجريبي)** — opens your store in a new tab, so you can order like a customer. The step counts once your first order arrives.
7. **Share link (شارك اللينك)** — copy your store's link, or send it on **WhatsApp**, **Facebook**, **Instagram** or **SMS**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the checklist">
    On your Home page, find **Get your store ready for its first order** near the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the next step">
    Press the button on the next step's card to open the page where you do it, then come back to your Home page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch your progress fill in">
    A step is marked **Done** when your store shows it's done, not when you click it, so it counts wherever in the dashboard you did it. **Hide steps** folds the card down to its title.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When all seven are done, the card says **Your store is ready for orders 🎉 (متجرك جاهز يستقبل طلبات 🎉)**, with **Copy your store link (انسخ رابط متجرك)**, **Delete the test order (امسح الأوردر التجريبي)** while your test order is still there, and **Hide (إخفاء)** to remove the card.

<Tip>
  While a step is left, **Watch how to set up your store (اتفرّج إزاي تجهّز متجرك)** on your Home page plays a short video, from choosing a design to your first order.
</Tip>

When you import a ready-made design, a full-screen **setup screen** takes you through making it yours, with a live preview of your store: your **logo**; your **colours** (a ready-made colour set, one of them built from your logo, or each colour yourself); your **images** (tap a picture in the preview to replace it); your **text** (tap any text to retype it, including your store's own words such as **Add to cart** and **Place order**: change one once and it changes everywhere); and your **fonts**. Before you import a design, the checklist's **Your logo** step opens this screen on the logo alone. Your products and delivery aren't part of it; the checklist carries them.

<Note>
  The checklist is a guide, not a lock. You can explore the dashboard freely and come back to any step later.
</Note>

### Your store link

Near the top of your Home page, **Your store link (لينك متجرك)** shows your store's address, ready to send to customers:

- Click the address to open your store in a new tab.
- **Copy link (انسخ اللينك)** copies it, ready to paste into a chat, a post or your bio. Copying it also completes the checklist's **Share link** step.
- **Share (شارك)** sends it straight to **WhatsApp**, **Facebook**, **Instagram** or **SMS** — the same options as the checklist's **Share link** step.

It's on every store's Home page and stays there after you finish the checklist, so your link is always one click away.

### Your store at a glance

Below the welcome, the dashboard home shows live numbers and highlights so you can see how your store is performing without opening any reports:

- **Store stats** — key totals for your store, including **revenue**, **orders**, **items sold**, and **average order value**.
- **Live visitors** — how many people are on your site right now.
- **Best-selling products** — the items customers are buying most.
- **New orders** — your most recent orders, so you can act on them quickly.

<Tip>
  For deeper reports, open **Analytics (التحليلات)** in the side menu for your sales over time ([Store analytics](/en/store/store-analytics)), and **Marketing (التسويق)** for where your visitors come from ([Site analytics](/en/marketing/site-analytics)).
</Tip>

## The left-side menu

A menu on the side of the dashboard groups everything into sections. Here's what each one is for:

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home page (الصفحة الرئيسية)** | The home screen — your setup checklist, your store link, store stats, and recent activity. |
| **Orders (الطلبات)** | Every order your store receives. See [Orders](/en/store/orders). |
| **Store (المتجر)** | **Shipping (الشحن)** — where you deliver and what it costs — and **Payments (المدفوعات)** — the payment methods your store offers. |
| **Products (المنتجات)** | Your products, their **categories (الأقسام)**, **product options (خيارات المنتجات)** and **reviews (التقييمات)**, and your **discounts (الخصومات)** — the coupon codes customers enter at checkout. |
| **Customers (العملاء)** | The people who bought from you, and the **customer messages (رسائل العملاء)** sent through your site's forms. |
| **Analytics (التحليلات)** | Your sales reports, one link per report — overview, products, revenue, orders, and more. See [Store analytics](/en/store/store-analytics). |
| **Branding & contact buttons (الهوية وأزرار التواصل)** | Your store's name, short description, and logo, plus the WhatsApp button, your social media icons, and the language switcher. |
| **Store design (تصميم المتجر)** | Your **pages (الصفحات)**, the **ready-made templates (القوالب الجاهزة)**, and your **popups (النوافذ المنبثقة)**. See [Popups](/en/site/popups). |
| **Content (المحتوى)** | Your **blog (المدونة)** and your **images & files (الصور والملفات)**. |
| **Marketing (التسويق)** | Grow your business: search visibility (SEO), visitor analytics, journeys, live events, and **Connect your ads (ربط الإعلانات)** for your ad pixels. |
| **Ask glowply (اسأل glowply)** | An assistant that answers your questions about running your store. See [Ask glowply](#ask-glowply). |
| **Settings (الإعدادات)** | General settings, translating your site, and your Glowply billing. |

<Note>
  Your pages are designed in the **Builder (البلدر)** — Glowply's drag-and-drop visual editor. You don't need to write any code.
</Note>

## Your logo

Your logo is set in **Branding & contact buttons (الهوية وأزرار التواصل)**, next to your store's name and short description. The setup checklist's **Your logo (اللوجو)** step takes you to it too.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your logo">
    Under **الشعار** (Logo), choose **رفع ملف** (Upload file) to upload it from your device, or **معرض الوسائط** (Media library) to pick one you've already uploaded. A PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Choose **حفظ التغييرات** (Save changes). Your logo shows in your store's header and footer, and as the small icon on the browser tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change it later, choose a new image the same way; **إزالة** (Remove) takes it off. Under **الشعار في الهيدر** (Logo in the header) you can set the logo's width and the page that opens when someone clicks it.

<Note>
  Glowply doesn't design logos for you. Until you have one, your store shows its name in the logo's place, so you can open your store without one and add it later.
</Note>

## Ask glowply

**Ask glowply (اسأل glowply)** is an assistant inside your dashboard. Type a question in Arabic or English — for example *"How do I add a new product?"* — and it answers from this guide.

- **From any page of your dashboard:** a round chat button sits in the bottom corner, just above the WhatsApp button, with a message beside it: **Chat with your AI assistant (تحدث مع مساعدك الذكي)**. Click the button or the message and the assistant opens in a panel over the page, so you can ask without leaving where you are. On a phone, the panel fills the screen; close it with the **X** at the top.
- **From your store itself:** while you browse your store signed in, the same button sits in the bottom corner. Only you and your team see it; your customers never do.
- **From the menu:** open **Ask glowply (اسأل glowply)** in the side menu, or choose it from the **Help** button at the top.

The message beside the button goes away once you open the assistant or close it with the small **X** next to it. The button stays where it is.

When an answer tells you to open a page of your dashboard — **Products**, **Discounts**, **Shipping**, and so on — the page's name is a link. Click it and you go straight there. Your conversation is kept while the browser tab stays open, so it's still there when you come back.

<Note>
  The assistant answers from the guide and can occasionally be wrong. For anything about your account, your subscription, or a payment, [contact support](/en/contact-support).
</Note>
