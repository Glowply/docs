Signing in to Glowply signs you in to **your own website**. You enter your email and password at glowply.com, and Glowply takes you straight to your site dashboard — the place where you build pages, run your store, and manage your settings. There's no second password to enter and no separate portal to pass through.

<Info>
  **What changed.** Glowply used to sign you in to an account portal at `/my-account/`, and you opened your site from there. That extra step is gone. Signing in now lands you directly in your site dashboard. Old `/my-account/` bookmarks still work — they simply forward you to your site.
</Info>

## Where to log in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open glowply.com">
    Go to [glowply.com](https://glowply.com) in any browser, on your phone or computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose “Log in”">
    Select the **Log in (تسجيل الدخول)** button at the top of the page, next to **Start free now**. On a phone it sits next to **Menu (القائمة)**. This opens your branded Glowply login page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign in with your email and password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Type the email address you used when you created your site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your password">
    Type the password you set during sign-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep me signed in (optional)">
    Turn on **Keep me signed in** so you don't have to log in again every time on this device. Leave it off if you're on a shared or public computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Select the login button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Use the same email you signed up with. Your email can't be changed by you — if you need a different one, [contact support](/en/contact-support).
</Note>

## What happens next

After you log in, you'll see a short loading screen that says **“Signing you in to your site.”** Glowply is creating a secure one-time sign-in link for your website in the background. It usually takes a moment, then your site dashboard opens.

<Steps>
  <Step title="A loading screen appears">
    You'll see the Glowply logo and a spinner. Nothing is required from you — just wait.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your site dashboard opens">
    You land in your own website's dashboard, already signed in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Once you're in your dashboard, bookmark it. Next time you can open your site directly, and only come back to glowply.com when you need to sign in again.
</Tip>

## Forgot your password?

If you can't remember your password, you can reset it from the login page — Glowply emails you a secure reset link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the forgot-password link">
    On the login page, select **Forgot password?**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Type the email address on your account, then submit the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Glowply sends a password-reset link to that email. Open the email and select the link.

    <Note>If you don't see it within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Choose a new password (at least 8 characters), then log in with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Pick a password that's at least 8 characters and easy for you to remember but hard for others to guess.
</Tip>

## Signing out

To sign out of Glowply, use the logout option in your site dashboard. You'll be returned to the Glowply sign-in page and can log in again any time.

## Still can't get in?

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My password isn't working">
    Make sure Caps Lock is off and there are no extra spaces. If you're still locked out, use **Forgot password?** to set a new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I didn't receive the reset email">
    Check your spam or junk folder, and confirm you entered the same email you used at sign-up. Reset emails can take a few minutes to arrive. If it still doesn't come through, [contact support](/en/contact-support).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I forgot which email I used">
    Try the email address you check most often. If you're not sure, [contact support](/en/contact-support) and the team can help you find your account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says I haven't launched my site yet">
    This means your account doesn't have a website attached to it — usually because sign-up wasn't finished. Select **Start now** on that screen to create your site, or follow [Create your site](/en/quickstart). If you believe your site already exists, [contact support](/en/contact-support).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says it couldn't sign me in to my site">
    This is usually temporary. Select **Try again** on that screen. If it keeps failing, wait a few minutes and try once more — and if it still won't go through, [contact support](/en/contact-support) with your site address.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The loading screen doesn't finish">
    Refresh the page to start a fresh sign-in. Each sign-in link can only be used once, so refreshing is the right fix rather than waiting. If it still doesn't complete, [contact support](/en/contact-support).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I haven't created an account yet">
    You need to sign up first. Follow [Create your site](/en/quickstart) to register and verify your phone, then come back here to log in.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Haven't verified your phone yet? Learn what to expect in [Account verification](/en/account-verification).
</Note>
