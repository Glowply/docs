Glowply is the fastest way for a small business to get a real website and online store. You sign up, verify your phone, and Glowply automatically builds a complete, hosted site for you at `yourname.myglowply.com` — with secure HTTPS already switched on. No coding, no hosting setup, no designer.

## Where you'll work

Everything happens in one place: your **site dashboard**. You sign in at glowply.com and Glowply takes you straight there.

## Who Glowply is for

- Sellers who currently sell on Instagram or WhatsApp and want a real store with a shopping cart
- Small businesses that need a professional website without hiring anyone
- Anyone who wants to be online today, not next month

## How billing works

Glowply runs on a **prepaid balance**. Credit is added to your account, and your monthly subscription renews automatically from that balance — no card is charged on a schedule. Every new account starts with a **free trial**.

<Note>
  Billing is currently moving into your site dashboard, under **Settings → Subscription & Billing**. Until it's connected, [contact support](/en/contact-support) to add credit or ask about a renewal. See [Your account & billing](/en/account/overview).
</Note>

<Note>
  Your site address is always `yourname.myglowply.com`. You can connect your own custom domain later through support.
</Note>
