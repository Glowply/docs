**Payments (الدفع)** is where you choose how your customers pay when they order. You'll find it in your **site dashboard** under **Store → Payments (المتجر ← الدفع)**. Turn a method on and it shows up at checkout right away; turn it off and it disappears.

<Info>
  This page is about how **your customers** pay you. Paying for your Glowply subscription is something else — see [Billing & payments](/en/account/billing-and-payments).
</Info>

## Two ways customers can pay

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Cash on delivery" icon="money-bill">
    The customer orders now and pays when the order arrives — in cash, or another way you accept on delivery.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Online payments" icon="credit-card">
    The customer pays when they order — by card, mobile wallet, or installments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can offer both, or just one.

## Cash on delivery

**Cash on delivery (الدفع عند الاستلام)** is the simplest option: the customer orders now and pays when the order reaches them. Use its switch to turn it on or off.

<Tip>
  Want every order paid upfront? Turn **Cash on delivery** off and keep online payments on. Customers then pay when they order.
</Tip>

## Online payments

**Online payments (الدفع أونلاين)** run through one payment provider at a time. In Egypt that provider is **Kashier**. When it shows **Active (نشط)**, its payment methods are listed underneath, each with its own switch:

| Method | What the customer pays with |
|---|---|
| **Card (البطاقة)** | A bank card |
| **Mobile Wallet (المحفظة الإلكترونية)** | A mobile wallet |
| **ValU**, **Souhoola (سهولة)**, **Aman (أمان)** | Installments |
| **Contact Now (كونتاكت)** | A Contact loan |

Turn off any method you don't want to offer. To stop online payments altogether, choose **Disable (إيقاف)** next to the provider.

<Note>
  **Configure (إعداد)** next to a method opens its detailed settings, where you can change the name and description customers see at checkout. Leave the connection settings (the merchant ID and keys) as they are — changing them can stop online payments from working.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Before you share your store, place a test order that you pay online — the setup checklist's **Test order** step is a good moment. If online payment doesn't go through, or you're not sure your store is set up to receive online payments, [contact support](/en/contact-support).
</Warning>

## What isn't available

- **Deposits or part payments.** A customer pays either the full amount online when they order, or on delivery. You can't collect a deposit first and the rest later.
- **Bank transfer** isn't offered as a payment method.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I ask customers for a deposit?">
    Not at the moment — a customer pays either the full amount online or on delivery. If you want orders paid before you ship them, turn **Cash on delivery** off so customers pay online when they order.

    *Also asked: “Can customers pay part of the price upfront?”*
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to install a payment gateway?">
    No. Payments are built into your store: you only choose which methods to offer here. One online provider can be active at a time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A payment method doesn't show at checkout. Why?">
    Check that its switch is on in **Store → Payments**. Changes apply right away, so reload the checkout page to see them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is this where I pay for my Glowply subscription?">
    No. Your subscription is paid from your prepaid wallet in **Settings → Subscription & Billing** — see [Billing & payments](/en/account/billing-and-payments).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
