New to Glowply or stuck on a quick question? Start here. These are the questions people ask most often, with short, plain answers. If you need step-by-step help, follow the links into the full guides — and if you can't find what you need, [contact support](/en/contact-support).

<Info>
  Glowply works in one place. You sign in at glowply.com and land straight in your **site dashboard**, where you build pages, run your store, and manage your billing. The old account portal at `/my-account/` has been retired — see [Your account & billing](/en/account/overview) for where each part went.
</Info>

## Questions store owners ask

<Note>
  These answers come from the questions store owners ask **Ask glowply (اسأل glowply)**, the assistant inside their dashboard. As new questions come in, we add them here.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I start?">
    Start on the **Home page (الصفحة الرئيسية)** of your site dashboard. The **Get your store ready for its first order** checklist walks you through seven steps in order: **Your design**, **Your logo**, **Categories**, **Products**, **Delivery**, **Test order**, and **Share link**. Click a step to open the place where you finish it.

    See [Site dashboard overview](/en/site/dashboard-overview).

    *Also asked: “What should I do first?”*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add a product?">
    In your site dashboard, go to **Products (المنتجات)** and choose **Add product (ضيف منتج)** — or use **Add Product** on your Home page. Add a photo, a name, and a price (the rest can wait), then publish it.

    See [Products](/en/store/products).

    *Also asked: “How do I put a product on my store?”*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I add many products at once?">
    Yes. At the top of your Products list, choose **Import (استيراد)** and upload a CSV file of your products (up to 300 MB), match its columns, and import. For products that are alike, choose **Duplicate (تكرار)** under one of them to get a draft copy, then change only what's different.

    Not sure which columns the file needs? Choose **Export (تصدير)** first and use that file as your template. See [Add many products at once](/en/store/products#add-many-products-at-once).

    *Also asked: “Can I upload all my products from a spreadsheet?”*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I hide or delete a product?">
    **To hide it:** open the product and choose **Switch to draft (حوّله لمسودة)**. It leaves your Store but stays saved under the **Drafts** tab, ready to publish again.

    **To delete it:** in your Products list, choose **Delete (حذف)** under the product. It moves to the **Trash** tab, where you can **Restore (استعادة)** it or **Delete Permanently (مسح نهائي)**. Deleting permanently can't be undone.

    See [Hide or delete a product](/en/store/products#hide-or-delete-a-product).

    *Also asked: “How do I take a product off my store?”*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I deleted a product or a page by mistake. Can I get it back?">
    Yes, as long as you didn't choose **Delete Permanently**. Deleted products and pages wait in the **Trash** tab of their list: open it and choose **Restore (استعادة)**.

    - A **product** comes back exactly as it was.
    - A **page** comes back as a **draft** — publish it again so visitors can see it.

    See [Hide or delete a product](/en/store/products#hide-or-delete-a-product) and [Delete a page](/en/site/pages#delete-a-page).

    *Also asked: “I deleted something by accident — how do I undo it?”*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I sell by weight?">
    Glowply doesn't price by the kilo or gram with the customer typing any amount. You can offer set weights instead: give the product an option such as **Weight** with values like *250 g*, *500 g*, and *1 kg*, and set a price for each.

    See [Product options](/en/store/attributes) and the variations steps in [Products](/en/store/products).

    *Also asked: “Can I set a price per kilo?”*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I ask customers for a deposit?">
    Not at the moment. A customer pays either the full amount online when they order, or on delivery. If you want orders paid before you ship them, turn **Cash on delivery** off in **Store → Payments** so customers pay online when they order.

    See [Payments](/en/store/payments).

    *Also asked: “Can customers pay part of the price upfront?”*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's my store's link, and how do I share it?">
    Your store's address is **`<name>.myglowply.com`** — the name you chose when you signed up (or your own domain, if you've connected one). Open it any time with **View your store (شوف متجرك)** at the top of your dashboard.

    To share it, open the last step of the checklist on your Home page, **Share link (شارك اللينك)**. Choose **Copy link**, or send it straight to **WhatsApp**, **Facebook**, **Instagram**, or **SMS**. Put the link in your Instagram and Facebook bio so anyone who visits your page can reach your store.

    To control how your link looks when it's shared, see [Social share previews](/en/marketing/seo/social-previews).

    *Also asked: “Where's my store link?” · “How do I send my store to people?”*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I make my store look better?">
    To change a page, go to **Store design → Pages (تصميم المتجر ← الصفحات)** and open it in **the Builder** to edit its text, images, and sections. For a whole new look, pick one of the **Ready-made templates (القوالب الجاهزة)** — but importing one replaces your current design (your products, orders, and content are kept), so it's best done early. Your logo and contact buttons are under **Branding & contact buttons (الهوية وأزرار التواصل)**.

    See [Editing with the Builder](/en/site/builder-overview) and [Template library](/en/site/templates-library).

    *Also asked: “How do I change my store's look?”*
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Glowply?">
    Glowply is a "website and online store as a service" built for small businesses and online sellers. You sign up, verify your phone, and Glowply automatically builds you a hosted website with a built-in store — including the address, secure HTTPS, and the store itself. You then manage everything from a simple dashboard, with no servers or technical setup to worry about.

    For the full picture, see [How Glowply works](/en/how-glowply-works).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need technical skills or coding to use it?">
    No. Glowply is made for people who have never built a website. Your site is created for you automatically, hosting and security are handled in the background, and you edit your pages visually in **the Builder** — no code required. If you can fill in a form and click around a dashboard, you can run your Glowply site.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I sign up?">
    Go to **glowply.com**, choose **Start now**, and fill in your site address, name, email, phone, and a password. You'll then verify your phone with a 6-digit code (sent on WhatsApp first, with an SMS fallback). After that, Glowply builds your site automatically and signs you in to your new dashboard.

    See [Quickstart](/en/quickstart) and [Account verification](/en/account-verification) for the details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What will my site address be?">
    Your site lives at **`<name>.myglowply.com`**, where `<name>` is the site address you chose when you signed up — for example, `cairo-flowers.myglowply.com`. The address must use lowercase English letters, numbers, and hyphens only, and it can't start or end with a hyphen. You get one site per account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use my own custom domain?">
    Yes. You always keep your `<name>.myglowply.com` address, and a custom domain (like `www.yourbrand.com`) can be mapped to your site on request. Custom domains aren't self-serve — [contact support](/en/contact-support) and we'll set it up for you. See [Site management](/en/account/site-management) for more.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does billing work?">
    Billing runs on a **prepaid wallet**. You top up your wallet in **Settings → Subscription & Billing (الإعدادات ← الاشتراك والفواتير)**, and your monthly subscription renews itself from that balance — no card is charged at renewal time. When you sign up, a **free trial** starts automatically. After the trial, and every month after that, the renewal amount comes out of your wallet. As long as it covers the renewal, your site keeps running.

    See [Subscription & renewals](/en/account/subscription) and [Billing & payments](/en/account/billing-and-payments).

    *Also asked: “How does my subscription renew?”*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I top up my wallet?">
    In your site dashboard, go to **Settings → Subscription & Billing**. Choose how much to add — just enough for your next renewal, several renewals ahead (one, three, or six), or a custom amount — then pay by bank card or mobile wallet. Your balance goes up as soon as the payment is confirmed. Only the store owner sees this page.

    See [Billing & payments](/en/account/billing-and-payments).

    *Also asked: “How do I add money to my wallet?”*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods can I use?">
    A **bank card** (Visa or Mastercard — activates instantly) or a **mobile wallet** (Vodafone Cash or Orange Cash — confirmed within a few minutes). Payments run on a secure page from a certified payment provider, and Glowply doesn't store your card details. All amounts are in Egyptian pounds (EGP). See [Billing & payments](/en/account/billing-and-payments).

    <Note>A card is only used when *you* choose to top up. Renewals always come from your wallet, never from a card automatically.</Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much does it cost?">
    Glowply is a single monthly plan, billed in Egyptian pounds (EGP). Prices aren't listed in this guide because they can change — see the current prices on [glowply.com](https://glowply.com/#pricing). Your own renewal amount is always shown in **Settings → Subscription & Billing**, and paying for several renewals ahead costs less per month (you can save up to 30%).

    *Also asked: “How much is the subscription?”*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I don't pay?">
    If a renewal comes due and your wallet doesn't cover it, your subscription becomes **past due** and your site may be paused. Glowply keeps retrying the renewal automatically — you don't have to trigger anything. As soon as your wallet covers it, the next retry succeeds, your status returns to **active**, and your site comes back.

    <Warning>While your subscription is past due, your site can be temporarily paused and visitors may not be able to reach it. Top up your wallet in **Settings → Subscription & Billing** as soon as you can so your site comes back.</Warning>

    Full details are in [Subscription & renewals](/en/account/subscription).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. A free trial subscription starts automatically the moment your site is created — there's nothing separate to buy. Nothing is charged during the trial. Before it ends, top up your wallet in **Settings → Subscription & Billing** so it covers your first renewal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get an invoice or receipt?">
    Yes. Every top-up and renewal is listed in the wallet & invoice log in **Settings → Subscription & Billing**, and each receipt is also emailed to you automatically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account & subscription

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    Go to **Settings → Subscription & Billing** and cancel from the bottom of the page. Your store keeps running until the end of the period you've already paid for. If you simply stop paying, an unpaid renewal leaves your subscription past due rather than cancelling it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change my plan or upgrade?">
    Glowply offers a single monthly plan, so there's no upgrade, downgrade, or plan switch to choose. If your needs change, [contact support](/en/contact-support) and we'll help.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I change my name, email, or phone?">
    All three go through support at the moment — the Account tab where you used to edit them lived in the retired portal. [Contact support](/en/contact-support) and we'll update them for you. See [Account settings](/en/account/account-settings).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I change my password?">
    Use **Forgot password?** on the Glowply login page. Glowply sends a reset link to your email — open it and set a new password. See [Account settings](/en/account/account-settings) and [Logging in](/en/logging-in).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where did the account portal go?">
    It's been retired. Signing in at glowply.com takes you straight into your own site dashboard, and billing lives there too, under **Settings → Subscription & Billing**. Old `/my-account/` links forward you automatically. See [Your account & billing](/en/account/overview).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Hosting, security & sign-up rules

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is hosting and SSL included?">
    Yes. Hosting is included, and your site is secured automatically with HTTPS (an SSL certificate). There's nothing to buy, install, or configure — it's all handled for you in the background. See [Site management](/en/account/site-management).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which phone numbers are supported at sign-up?">
    Sign-up supports **Egyptian phone numbers**. Your verification code is sent on WhatsApp first, with an SMS fallback. Each phone number can be used for one trial sign-up, and your phone and email must be unique to your account. See [Account verification](/en/account-verification).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have more than one site?">
    Each account includes one `<name>.myglowply.com` site. If you need an additional site, [contact support](/en/contact-support) and we'll advise on the best option for you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting help

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get support?">
    For a quick answer, open **Ask glowply (اسأل glowply)** in your dashboard — the round button in the bottom corner, or the **Help** menu — and ask in your own words. It answers from this guide.

    To reach a person, there are three easy ways:

    - **Live chat** — click the chat bubble (or **Help**) on any page.
    - **WhatsApp** — message our support number.
    - **Email** — write to **support@myglowply.com**.

    Use support in particular to change your email or request a custom domain. See [Contact support](/en/contact-support).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Something isn't working — what should I check first?">
    Start with [Troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting) for the most common fixes. If your site is offline, an unpaid renewal is a common cause — a past-due subscription can pause your site, so check your wallet in **Settings → Subscription & Billing** and top it up. If that's not it, [contact support](/en/contact-support) with your site address and we'll check it and help.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
