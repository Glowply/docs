New to Glowply or stuck on a quick question? Start here. These are the questions people ask most often, with short, plain answers. If you need step-by-step help, follow the links into the full guides — and if you can't find what you need, [contact support](/en/contact-support).

<Info>
  Glowply now works in one place. You sign in at glowply.com and land straight in your **site dashboard**, where you build pages and run your store. The old account portal at `/my-account/` has been retired — see [Your account & billing](/en/account/overview) for where each part went.
</Info>

## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Glowply?">
    Glowply is a "website and online store as a service" built for small businesses and online sellers. You sign up, verify your phone, and Glowply automatically builds you a hosted website with a built-in store — including the address, secure HTTPS, and the store itself. You then manage everything from a simple dashboard, with no servers or technical setup to worry about.

    For the full picture, see [How Glowply works](/en/how-glowply-works).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need technical skills or coding to use it?">
    No. Glowply is made for people who have never built a website. Your site is created for you automatically, hosting and security are handled in the background, and you edit your pages visually in **the Builder** — no code required. If you can fill in a form and click around a dashboard, you can run your Glowply site.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I sign up?">
    Go to **glowply.com**, choose **Start now**, and fill in your site address, name, email, phone, and a password. You'll then verify your phone with a 6-digit code (sent on WhatsApp first, with an SMS fallback). After that, Glowply builds your site automatically and signs you in to your new dashboard.

    See [Quickstart](/en/quickstart) and [Account verification](/en/account-verification) for the details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What will my site address be?">
    Your site lives at **`<name>.myglowply.com`**, where `<name>` is the site address you chose when you signed up — for example, `cairo-flowers.myglowply.com`. The address must use lowercase English letters, numbers, and hyphens only, and it can't start or end with a hyphen. You get one site per account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use my own custom domain?">
    Yes. You always keep your `<name>.myglowply.com` address, and a custom domain (like `www.yourbrand.com`) can be mapped to your site on request. Custom domains aren't self-serve — [contact support](/en/contact-support) and we'll set it up for you. See [Site management](/en/account/site-management) for more.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does billing work?">
    Billing runs on a **prepaid balance**. Credit is added to your account, and your monthly subscription renews itself automatically from that balance — there's no card charged at renewal time. When you sign up, a **free trial** starts automatically. After the trial, and at the end of every period after that, the renewal amount is taken from your balance. As long as it covers the renewal, your site keeps running.

    See [Subscription & renewals](/en/account/subscription) and [Billing & payments](/en/account/billing-and-payments).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add credit or pay right now?">
    [Contact support](/en/contact-support) and the team will arrange it with you. Adding credit isn't self-serve at the moment: the old top-up screen was retired with the account portal, and its replacement in your site dashboard isn't connected yet.

    <Note>If you already see a **Subscription & Billing** page in your dashboard with a yellow **“Preview — sample data”** notice, that page isn't live. The plan, dates, and amounts on it are placeholder examples, not your real billing.</Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods can I use?">
    Card, mobile wallet, and InstaPay are all supported — let support know which you'd prefer. Payments run on a secure hosted page provided by our payment partner, and card payments are protected with 3-D Secure. All amounts are in Egyptian pounds (EGP). See [Billing & payments](/en/account/billing-and-payments).

    <Note>A card is only used when *you* choose to add credit. Renewals are always taken from your prepaid balance, never from a card automatically.</Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much does it cost?">
    Glowply is a single monthly plan, billed in Egyptian pounds (EGP). Longer renewal periods cost less per month. Prices aren't listed here because they can change — for current pricing see glowply.com, and [contact support](/en/contact-support) for your exact renewal amount.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I don't pay?">
    If a renewal comes due and your balance doesn't cover it, your subscription becomes **past due** and your site may be paused. Glowply keeps retrying the renewal automatically — you don't have to trigger anything. As soon as the balance covers it, the next retry succeeds, your status returns to **active**, and your site comes back.

    <Warning>While your subscription is past due, your site can be temporarily paused and visitors may not be able to reach it. [Contact support](/en/contact-support) as soon as you can so we can clear it.</Warning>

    Full details are in [Subscription & renewals](/en/account/subscription).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. A free trial subscription starts automatically the moment your site is created — there's nothing separate to buy. Nothing is charged during the trial. Before it ends, get in touch so your balance covers the first renewal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get an invoice or receipt?">
    Yes — [contact support](/en/contact-support) and we'll send you a copy. Self-serve invoice history is coming with the new billing screen in your dashboard.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account & subscription

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    Cancelling goes through support — it's never been self-serve. [Contact support](/en/contact-support) and we'll take care of it for you. If you simply stop paying, an unpaid renewal will leave your subscription past due rather than cancelling it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change my plan or upgrade?">
    Glowply offers a single monthly plan, so there's no upgrade, downgrade, or plan switch to choose. If your needs change, [contact support](/en/contact-support) and we'll help.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I change my name, email, or phone?">
    All three go through support at the moment — the Account tab where you used to edit them lived in the retired portal. [Contact support](/en/contact-support) and we'll update them for you. See [Account settings](/en/account/account-settings).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I change my password?">
    Use **Forgot password?** on the Glowply login page. Glowply sends a reset link to your email — open it and set a new password. See [Account settings](/en/account/account-settings) and [Logging in](/en/logging-in).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where did the account portal go?">
    It's been retired. Signing in at glowply.com now takes you straight into your own site dashboard, and billing is moving there too. Old `/my-account/` links forward you automatically. See [Your account & billing](/en/account/overview).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Hosting, security & sign-up rules

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is hosting and SSL included?">
    Yes. Hosting is included, and your site is secured automatically with HTTPS (an SSL certificate). There's nothing to buy, install, or configure — it's all handled for you in the background. See [Site management](/en/account/site-management).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which phone numbers are supported at sign-up?">
    Sign-up supports **Egyptian phone numbers**. Your verification code is sent on WhatsApp first, with an SMS fallback. Each phone number can be used for one trial sign-up, and your phone and email must be unique to your account. See [Account verification](/en/account-verification).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have more than one site?">
    Each account includes one `<name>.myglowply.com` site. If you need an additional site, [contact support](/en/contact-support) and we'll advise on the best option for you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting help

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get support?">
    There are three easy ways to reach us:

    - **Live chat** — click the chat bubble (or **Help**) on any page.
    - **WhatsApp** — message our support number.
    - **Email** — write to **support@myglowply.com**.

    Use support in particular to cancel or change your subscription, change your email, or request a custom domain. See [Contact support](/en/contact-support).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Something isn't working — what should I check first?">
    Start with [Troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting) for the most common fixes. If your site is offline, an unpaid renewal is a common cause — a past-due subscription can pause your site. [Contact support](/en/contact-support) with your site address and we'll check it and help.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
