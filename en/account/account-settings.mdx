Your **Glowply account** is the email and password you sign in with at glowply.com. It's separate from the content of your website: your account identifies *you*, while your site dashboard is where your pages, products, and settings live.

<Info>
  **What changed.** These details used to be editable in the **Account** tab of the account portal. That portal has been retired, so name, email, and phone changes now go through support. Password resets still work the same way, from the login page.
</Info>

## Your details

| Detail | What it is | How to change it |
|---|---|---|
| **First and last name** | Your name on the account and in support conversations | [Contact support](/en/contact-support) |
| **Email** | The address you sign in with, and where important notices go | [Contact support](/en/contact-support) |
| **Phone** | The mobile number linked to your account | [Contact support](/en/contact-support) |
| **Password** | What you sign in with | Reset it yourself — see below |

<Note>
  Email has always been fixed to keep your account identity stable across Glowply and your site. Name and phone are handled by support for now, until account settings return to your dashboard.
</Note>

## Change your password

You reset your password from the Glowply login page — Glowply emails you a secure reset link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the login page">
    Open [glowply.com](https://glowply.com) and choose **Login to your account (تسجيل الدخول)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose “Forgot password?”">
    Select **Forgot password?** below the sign-in form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Type the email address on your account and submit the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    Glowply sends a reset link to that address. Open it and follow the link.

    <Note>If you don't see it within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Choose a new password (at least 8 characters), then sign in with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The reset email goes to your account's email address. If you no longer have access to that inbox, [contact support](/en/contact-support) before requesting a reset — your email needs to be updated first.
</Warning>

## Signing out

Use the logout option in your site dashboard to end your session. You'll be returned to the Glowply sign-in page and can [log in](/en/logging-in) again any time.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I change my name or phone myself right now?">
    Those fields lived in the account portal, which has been retired. Until account settings return to your site dashboard, [contact support](/en/contact-support) and the team will update them for you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I change my email myself?">
    Your email is tied to your account identity. Changing it through support keeps everything connected — your sign-in, your notices, and your site — so nothing gets out of sync.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my Glowply password the same as my site password?">
    You only need one. You sign in at glowply.com with your Glowply password, and Glowply signs you in to your site automatically — there's no second password to remember.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My password reset email didn't arrive.">
    Give it a few minutes and check your spam or junk folder. Make sure you're checking the inbox for your account's email address. If it still doesn't arrive, [contact support](/en/contact-support).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I changed my name — does my site address change too?">
    No. Your name is a personal detail on your account. Your site address (`yourname.myglowply.com`) is separate and stays the same. See [Site management](/en/account/site-management).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add a second person to my account?">
    Your Glowply account belongs to one account holder. You can add users to your website itself from the **Users** section of your site dashboard — that controls access to your site, not to your Glowply billing. For help, [contact support](/en/contact-support).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
