Every Glowply site comes with its own web address, HTTPS security, and the option of a custom domain. This page covers those — the parts of your site that sit outside the Builder.

<Info>
  **What changed.** These details used to live in the **Site management** tab of the account portal. That portal has been retired — you now arrive in your site dashboard directly when you sign in, and the links below are at the top of it.
</Info>

## Your site address

Every Glowply site gets a free address in the form `yourname.myglowply.com`. You chose the `yourname` part during sign-up, and it stays with your account.

<Note>
  Your `yourname.myglowply.com` address is set when your site is created and can't be edited afterwards. If you need a different web address, the usual path is to request a custom domain — see below.
</Note>

## View your live site and store

The green **View your store (شوف متجرك)** button at the top of your site dashboard opens your store in a new tab, exactly as your customers see it. It's there on every screen, on your phone too, and your dashboard stays open in its own tab.

<Tip>
  Use it to check your work. Make your edits in the **Builder**, save them, then press **View your store** to see the result the way a real visitor would.
</Tip>

## SSL and HTTPS

Your site is **secured automatically** with HTTPS. The padlock and the `https://` in your address are handled for you — there's nothing to set up, renew, or configure.

<Note>
  SSL (the security certificate behind HTTPS) is included and managed by Glowply for every site. You don't need to buy, install, or renew a certificate — your site is encrypted from day one.
</Note>

## Custom domain

If you'd like to use your own web address — such as `yourbrand.com` — you can request a **custom domain (النطاق المخصص)**. Our team maps it to your site for you; it isn't something you set up yourself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Have your domain ready">
    Make sure you own the domain you want to use (for example, you bought `yourbrand.com` from a domain registrar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    Reach out through [support](/en/contact-support) and tell us the domain you'd like to connect to your site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We map it for you">
    Our team connects your custom domain to your Glowply site. We'll let you know once it's live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Custom domains are set up by request — they're not self-serve. You keep your original `yourname.myglowply.com` address as well, and both can point to the same website.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I build and edit my site?">
    In your site dashboard, which opens automatically when you sign in at glowply.com. See [Dashboard overview](/en/site/dashboard-overview) and [Builder overview](/en/site/builder-overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to set up SSL or HTTPS?">
    No. Your site is secured automatically with HTTPS. There's nothing to install, buy, or renew — Glowply handles it for every site.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my own domain name?">
    Yes. Request a custom domain through [support](/en/contact-support) and our team will map it to your site. You keep your `yourname.myglowply.com` address as well.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my yourname.myglowply.com address?">
    Your address is set when your site is created. If you need a different one, the usual path is to request a custom domain through [support](/en/contact-support).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My site is offline — what should I check?">
    An unpaid renewal can pause a site. See [Subscription & renewals](/en/account/subscription), and [contact support](/en/contact-support) with your site address if you're unsure.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
