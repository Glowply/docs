Your **subscription** is what keeps your Glowply site and store online. It starts as a **free trial** the moment your site is created — there's no separate purchase to make. After the trial, it renews itself automatically from your prepaid Glowply balance.

<Info>
  Your subscription runs in the background — it isn't something you have to operate. This page explains what happens automatically and when you need to get in touch.
</Info>

<Note>
  **Where you'll see this.** Your plan, renewal date, and invoice history are moving to **Settings → Subscription & Billing** in your site dashboard. That screen isn't connected yet — see [Your account & billing](/en/account/overview) for what to expect. In the meantime, [contact support](/en/contact-support) for your current status or renewal date.
</Note>

## How the free trial works

When your site is built, Glowply creates a free trial subscription for you automatically. During the trial:

- Your site and store are fully online and usable.
- **Nothing is charged.**
- The trial runs for a set period, then your first renewal becomes due.

Before the trial ends, your balance needs enough credit to cover that first renewal. [Contact support](/en/contact-support) to arrange it.

## Subscription statuses

Your subscription is always in one of these states:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Trialing (فترة تجريبية)** | Your free trial is active. The site is live and nothing is charged yet. |
| **Active (نشط)** | Your subscription is paid and current. It renews again at the end of the period. |
| **Past due (متأخر الدفع)** | A renewal couldn't be taken because your balance was too low. |
| **Cancelled (ملغي)** | The subscription has been ended. Contact support if you'd like to start again. |

## How renewals are paid

Glowply doesn't charge a card on a schedule. Every renewal is taken from your **prepaid balance** — credit that's added to your account ahead of time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="A period ends">
    At the end of your trial, and again at the end of each period after that, a renewal becomes due.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The amount is taken from your balance">
    Glowply deducts the renewal amount automatically. There's no card charge at renewal time and nothing for you to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enough balance → your site stays active">
    The renewal succeeds, your status stays **active**, and your period moves forward to the next renewal date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Not enough balance → past due">
    The renewal can't complete and your subscription becomes **past due**. Glowply keeps retrying automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What "past due" means

**Past due** means a renewal came due but your balance didn't cover it. Here's what to expect:

- Glowply **retries the renewal automatically** on a regular schedule — you don't have to trigger anything.
- As soon as your balance covers the amount, the next retry succeeds and your status returns to **active**.
- While past due, **your site may be paused** until the balance is topped up.

<Warning>
  While your subscription is past due, your site can be temporarily paused — visitors may not be able to reach it. Get in touch as soon as you can so the team can clear it and bring your site back.
</Warning>

## Recover from past due

Adding credit isn't self-serve at the moment, so recovery goes through our team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    Reach out through [support](/en/contact-support) with your site address and let us know your site is past due.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We arrange the payment with you">
    The team will confirm the amount due and take you through paying it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your subscription renews">
    Once your balance covers the renewal, the next automatic retry completes it. Your status returns to **active** and your site comes back — there's nothing for you to press.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your site doesn't come back within a little while after a payment is confirmed, [contact support](/en/contact-support) again and we'll check it for you.
</Tip>

## Changing or cancelling your plan

Glowply offers one monthly plan, so there's no upgrade or downgrade to choose. Cancellations and plan changes go through support — they've never been self-serve.

<Card title="Contact support" icon="headset" href="/en/contact-support">
  Email **support@myglowply.com**, start a live chat, or message us on WhatsApp to cancel or change your subscription.
</Card>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will my card be charged when my subscription renews?">
    No. Renewals are taken from your prepaid Glowply balance, never from a card automatically. A card is only involved when you choose to add credit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I check my renewal date right now?">
    [Contact support](/en/contact-support) and we'll tell you. The **Subscription & Billing** page in your dashboard isn't connected yet, so the date shown there is a placeholder, not your real renewal date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long do I have to fix a past-due subscription?">
    There's no single deadline you have to hit by hand — Glowply keeps retrying on a schedule. The sooner the balance is topped up, the sooner the next retry succeeds. Your site may be paused while past due, so it's best to get in touch quickly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to do anything to renew once the balance is topped up?">
    No. Renewal is automatic. The next retry renews your subscription and returns your status to **active** on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I just stop paying?">
    An unpaid renewal leaves your subscription past due rather than cancelling it, and your site can be paused. To formally cancel, [contact support](/en/contact-support).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
