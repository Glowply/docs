Your **subscription** is what keeps your Glowply site and store online. It starts as a **free trial** the moment your site is created — there's no separate purchase to make. After the trial, it renews itself automatically from your prepaid **wallet balance**.

<Info>
  Your subscription runs in the background — it isn't something you have to operate. You can see and manage it any time in **Settings → Subscription & Billing (الإعدادات ← الاشتراك والفواتير)** in your site dashboard.
</Info>

<Note>
  **Where you'll see this.** Your plan, renewal, wallet balance, and invoice history all live on one screen: **Settings → Subscription & Billing**. It's marked **Store owner only**, so only the store owner sees it.
</Note>

## How the free trial works

When your site is built, Glowply creates a free trial subscription for you automatically. During the trial:

- Your site and store are fully online and usable.
- **Nothing is charged.**
- The trial runs for a set period, then your first renewal becomes due.

Before the trial ends, top up your wallet with enough credit to cover that first renewal — you can do it yourself in **Settings → Subscription & Billing**. See [Billing & payments](/en/account/billing-and-payments).

## Subscription statuses

Your subscription is always in one of these states:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Trialing (فترة تجريبية)** | Your free trial is active. The site is live and nothing is charged yet. |
| **Active (نشط)** | Your subscription is paid and current. It renews again at the end of the period. |
| **Past due (متأخر الدفع)** | A renewal couldn't be taken because your wallet balance was too low. |
| **Cancelled (ملغي)** | The subscription has been ended. Your store runs until the end of the paid period. |

## How renewals are paid

Glowply doesn't charge a card on a schedule. Every renewal is taken from your **wallet balance** — prepaid credit you top up ahead of time. Renewals are monthly, and the amount comes out of your wallet automatically, so you don't lose any days.

<Steps>
  <Step title="A period ends">
    At the end of your trial, and again at the end of each month after that, a renewal becomes due.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The amount is taken from your wallet">
    Glowply deducts the renewal amount automatically. There's no card charge at renewal time and nothing for you to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enough balance → your site stays active">
    The renewal succeeds, your status stays **active**, and your period moves forward to the next renewal date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Not enough balance → past due">
    The renewal can't complete and your subscription becomes **past due**. Glowply keeps retrying automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What "past due" means

**Past due** means a renewal came due but your wallet didn't cover it. Here's what to expect:

- Glowply **retries the renewal automatically** on a regular schedule — you don't have to trigger anything.
- As soon as your wallet covers the amount, the next retry succeeds and your status returns to **active**.
- While past due, **your site may be paused** until the wallet is topped up.

<Warning>
  While your subscription is past due, your site can be temporarily paused — visitors may not be able to reach it. Top up your wallet as soon as you can so your site comes back.
</Warning>

## Recover from past due

Topping up is self-serve, so you can clear a past-due subscription yourself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subscription & Billing">
    In your site dashboard, go to **Settings → Subscription & Billing**. You'll see your wallet balance and how much your next renewal needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Top up your wallet">
    Add enough credit to cover the renewal — by bank card or mobile wallet. See [Billing & payments](/en/account/billing-and-payments).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your subscription renews">
    Once your wallet covers the renewal, the next automatic retry completes it. Your status returns to **active** and your site comes back — there's nothing else to press.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your site doesn't come back within a little while after you top up, [contact support](/en/contact-support) and we'll check it for you.
</Tip>

## Changing or cancelling your plan

Glowply offers one monthly plan, so there's no upgrade or downgrade to choose. You can **cancel anytime** from **Settings → Subscription & Billing** — your store keeps running until the end of the period you've already paid for.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will my card be charged when my subscription renews?">
    No. Renewals are taken from your prepaid wallet balance, never from a card automatically. A card is only involved when you choose to top up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I check my renewal date?">
    Open **Settings → Subscription & Billing** in your site dashboard — your plan, renewal, and wallet balance are all shown there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long do I have to fix a past-due subscription?">
    There's no single deadline you have to hit by hand — Glowply keeps retrying on a schedule. The sooner your wallet is topped up, the sooner the next retry succeeds. Your site may be paused while past due, so it's best to top up quickly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to do anything to renew once my wallet is topped up?">
    No. Renewal is automatic. The next retry renews your subscription and returns your status to **active** on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I just stop paying?">
    An unpaid renewal leaves your subscription past due rather than cancelling it, and your site can be paused. To end it properly, cancel from **Settings → Subscription & Billing**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
