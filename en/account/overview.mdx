Glowply used to give you two places to work in: an **account portal** on glowply.com for billing, and your **site dashboard** for building. That's now one place. You sign in at glowply.com, and everything you manage — your store, your pages, and your billing — happens inside **your own site dashboard**.

<Info>
  **What changed.** The account portal at `/my-account/` has been retired. glowply.com is now just the front door — you sign in there and Glowply takes you straight to your site. Old `/my-account/` links still work and forward you to your site dashboard.
</Info>

## Where your old portal tabs went

If you used the account portal before, here's where each part lives now.

| What you used to do | Where it is now |
|---|---|
| Open your site dashboard | You land there automatically when you sign in |
| Visit your live site | The green **View your store (شوف متجرك)** button at the top of your site dashboard |
| Check your subscription and renewal | **Settings → Subscription & Billing (الإعدادات ← الاشتراك والفواتير)** |
| Top up your balance | **Settings → Subscription & Billing** |
| View your transactions | **Settings → Subscription & Billing**, in the wallet & invoice log |
| Update your name, phone, or password | See [Account settings](/en/account/account-settings) |
| Your domain and site address | See [Site management](/en/account/site-management) |

## Billing lives in your dashboard

Billing is now part of your site dashboard, under **Settings → Subscription & Billing**. That one screen shows your plan and renewal, your wallet balance, your payment options, and your invoice history — all without leaving your site.

<Note>
  Only the store owner sees this page — it's marked **Store owner only (لصاحب المتجر فقط)** and doesn't appear for other users you add to your site.
</Note>
