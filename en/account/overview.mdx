Glowply used to give you two places to work in: an **account portal** on glowply.com for billing, and your **site dashboard** for building. That's now one place. You sign in at glowply.com, and everything you manage happens inside **your own site dashboard**.

<Info>
  **What changed.** The account portal at `/my-account/` has been retired. glowply.com is now just the front door — you sign in there and Glowply takes you straight to your site. Old `/my-account/` links still work and forward you to your site dashboard.
</Info>

## Where your old portal tabs went

If you used the account portal before, here's where each part lives now.

| What you used to do | Where it is now |
|---|---|
| Open your site dashboard | You land there automatically when you sign in |
| Visit your live site | The green **View your store** button at the top of your site dashboard |
| Check your subscription and renewal | Moving to **Settings → Subscription & Billing** — see below |
| Top up your balance | Moving to **Settings → Subscription & Billing** — see below |
| View your transactions | Moving to **Settings → Subscription & Billing** — see below |
| Update your name, phone, or password | See [Account settings](/en/account/account-settings) |
| Your domain and site address | See [Site management](/en/account/site-management) |

## Billing is moving to your site

Billing is being moved out of glowply.com and into your own site dashboard, under **Settings → Subscription & Billing**. When it's live, that single screen will show your plan and renewal date, let you renew early, choose a renewal period, pay, and read your invoice history — all without leaving your site.

<Warning>
  **Not connected yet.** You may already see a **Subscription & Billing** page in your dashboard with a yellow **“Preview — sample data”** notice at the top. That page is still being connected to your real account. The plan, dates, amounts, and invoices shown on it are **placeholder examples, not your actual billing**, and its buttons don't do anything yet. Please don't rely on what it shows.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Only the store owner sees this page — it's marked **Store owner only** and doesn't appear for other users you add to your site.
</Note>

## How to handle billing today

Until that screen is connected, billing is handled by our team. Your subscription still renews automatically in the background — see [Subscription & renewals](/en/account/subscription) for how that works.
