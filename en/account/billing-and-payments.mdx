Glowply runs on a **prepaid wallet**. You top up your wallet balance, and each renewal is taken from it automatically — there's no card charged on a schedule. Keep enough credit and your site simply stays online.

You manage all of this yourself in **Settings → Subscription & Billing (الإعدادات ← الاشتراك والفواتير)** in your site dashboard. It's **Store owner only**.

## How paying works

<Steps>
  <Step title="You top up your wallet">
    You add credit to your wallet balance by bank card or mobile wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Renewals draw from your wallet">
    When a renewal comes due, the amount is deducted from your wallet automatically. Nothing is charged to a card at renewal time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your site stays online">
    As long as your wallet covers each renewal, nothing interrupts your site. See [Subscription & renewals](/en/account/subscription).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All amounts are in Egyptian pounds (**EGP / ج.م**). Exact prices aren't listed here because they can change — your live renewal amount and top-up options are shown on the **Subscription & Billing** screen.
</Note>

## Topping up your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subscription & Billing">
    In your site dashboard, go to **Settings → Subscription & Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how much to add">
    Top up the exact amount to cover your next renewal, buy several renewals ahead (one, three, or six), or enter a custom amount. Paying further ahead costs less per month — you can **save up to 30%**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a payment method and pay">
    Pay by **bank card** (Visa or Mastercard — activates instantly) or **mobile wallet** (Vodafone Cash or Orange Cash — confirmed within a few minutes). Your wallet balance goes up as soon as the payment is confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You don't have to wait for your wallet to run low. **Renew early** by topping up — the credit sits in your wallet and the next renewal draws from it, so you never lose days.
</Tip>

## Your wallet & invoice log

Every top-up and every renewal is listed in the **wallet & invoice log** on the Subscription & Billing screen, with the date, what happened, the amount, and your running wallet balance. Every receipt is also emailed to you automatically.

## Is it secure?

Yes. When you pay Glowply, the payment runs on a secure hosted page provided by a certified payment provider, and card payments add a confirmation from your bank — a quick verification step, such as a one-time code sent to your phone.

- Your full card details are entered on the payment provider's page, **not stored by Glowply**.
- Your connection is always secured with HTTPS.
- Your wallet balance only changes once a payment is genuinely confirmed.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What currency are payments in?">
    All payments are in Egyptian pounds (**EGP / ج.م**).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What can I pay with?">
    A **bank card** (Visa or Mastercard) or a **mobile wallet** (Vodafone Cash or Orange Cash). Card payments activate instantly; mobile-wallet payments are confirmed within a few minutes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will my card be charged automatically?">
    No. A card is only ever used when *you* choose to top up. Renewals come out of your prepaid wallet balance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My card was declined. Was I charged?">
    No. A declined payment never charges your card and never changes your wallet balance. Try again, or use another method.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get an invoice or receipt?">
    Yes. Every top-up and renewal appears in your wallet & invoice log, and each receipt is emailed to you automatically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?">
    Your wallet credit keeps your subscription active and isn't designed to be withdrawn. If you topped up by mistake or have a billing question, [contact support](/en/contact-support).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to top up a fixed amount?">
    No. Cover just your next renewal, buy several renewals ahead, or enter any custom amount within the range shown on the top-up screen.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
