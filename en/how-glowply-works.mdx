Glowply gives you one place to work: your **site dashboard**. You sign in at glowply.com, and Glowply takes you straight into it. Pages, products, orders, settings — it's all there.

This page is a map. It shows how you get in and the order things happen in, from sign-up to a live site.

<Info>
  **What changed.** Glowply used to have a separate account portal at `/my-account/` for billing, and you opened your site from it. That portal has been retired. glowply.com is now just the front door, and your site dashboard is where you work. Old `/my-account/` links forward you there automatically.
</Info>

## Signing in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to glowply.com">
    Choose **Login to your account (تسجيل الدخول)** and enter your email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Glowply signs you in to your site">
    A short loading screen appears while Glowply creates a secure one-time sign-in link for your website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You land in your dashboard">
    Your site dashboard opens, already signed in. There's no second password to enter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Bookmark your dashboard once you're in. You'll only need to go back to glowply.com when you have to sign in again.
</Tip>

## Your Glowply lifecycle

Here's the journey from the moment you sign up to a working website and store.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Create your account at `glowply.com`. You choose your site address (the part before `.myglowply.com`) and enter your name, email, phone, and a password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your phone">
    Glowply sends a 6-digit code to your phone — on WhatsApp first, with SMS as a fallback. Enter the code to confirm it's really you. See [account verification](/en/account-verification) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your site is built automatically">
    As soon as your phone is verified, Glowply builds your website and store for you at `yourname.myglowply.com`, with secure HTTPS already switched on. This takes only a moment — there's nothing to set up yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A free trial starts">
    Your subscription begins as a **free trial**, so your site is live right away and nothing is charged up front.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your site">
    You land in your site dashboard. Add your pages with the **Builder** and your products in the **Store**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How billing works, in short

Glowply runs on a **prepaid balance**. Credit is added to your account, and your monthly subscription renews itself from that balance — there's no card charged at renewal time.

- Every new account begins with a **free trial**, so nothing is charged up front.
- Before the trial ends, your balance needs enough credit to cover the first renewal.
- After that, each renewal is taken from the balance automatically. Keep enough credit and your site simply stays online.

<Warning>
  If your balance is too low when a renewal is due, your subscription becomes **past due** and your site can be paused until it's topped up.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **Billing is moving.** It's being built into your site dashboard, under **Settings → Subscription & Billing**. That screen isn't connected yet — until it is, [contact support](/en/contact-support) to add credit, settle a renewal, or ask for an invoice.
</Note>
